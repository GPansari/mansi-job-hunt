## Legend — What Each Column Captures

- **Company / Team**: employer and specific group (e.g. DEMPA, ART Advance Methods) — identifies who the actual hiring manager likely is.
- **Role / Location / Req ID**: exact title, site, and the company's internal requisition number — use the Req ID to re-search their own portal and confirm a role is still live.
- **Seniority / Key Skills**: filters for fit against Mansi's Lead Systems Engineer level and her actual toolset (Cameo, MATLAB, Simulink, DOORS, SysML).
- **Link / Source**: direct apply link, flagged Official (company's own ATS/Workday) vs Unverified/third-party mirror — mirrors can go stale after the real posting closes.
- **Fit (1-5)**: quick gut-check ranking of match to her background and seniority.
- **Status**: Not Applied / Applied / Screening / Interview / Offer / Rejected / Watching — update this as she progresses.
- **Next Action**: what needs to happen next — tailor resume, verify live status, find hiring manager, etc.
