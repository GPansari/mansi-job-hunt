**Research depth note (updated):** table now includes a **Relevance (1-5)** column and is sorted by it, highest first. Score reflects three factors together: confirmed Bangalore presence (biggest weight), funding/scale (company size and financial health), and role relevance (direct UAV/aircraft systems-engineering fit scores higher than space/satellite, manufacturing, agri, or logistics/software work, which are treated as adjacent). A company can score high without confirmed Bangalore presence if it's exceptionally well-funded and directly relevant (e.g. Raphe mPhibr at approx. $154M) - those are flagged for a direct check rather than excluded. 22 of 45 companies are funding/revenue-verified; the rest (mostly the 17 micro-companies with no public funding/revenue data across two research passes) sit at the bottom, scored 1, and are marked N/A/Deprioritized so they aren't re-searched without new information surfacing.
